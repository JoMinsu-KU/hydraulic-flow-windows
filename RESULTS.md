# Results and provenance

## Sources and units

The cycle assignments, phase means and predictions are derived from N. Helwig, E. Pignanelli and A. Schütze's *Condition Monitoring of Hydraulic Systems*, distributed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) by [UCI](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5CW21). The same test rig is documented in the [ZeMA deposit](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.1323611). The full raw signals are available from those providers. This repository includes derived window sums, cycle and condition labels, and 1,200 measured FS1 samples (four boundary readings in each of 300 exploratory cycles). A stored column `qJ_integer` is the sum of 50 raw samples in millionths of a liter per minute. Divide by 50,000,000 to recover the window mean in L/min. The group columns give cooler condition, valve condition, and accumulator pressure. The split column uses `fit`, `pilot`, `confirm`, and `unstable` for training, exploration, held-out test, and stability-flagged cycles.

The separate PONAR results use [Rojek and Blachnik's study](https://doi.org/10.3390/app14167183) and the [authors' valve-plate data release](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mbjunior/valve-plate-failure-prediction-in-hydraulic-pumps). The label and unit of evaluation are specific to that dataset. The PONAR records here contain timestamps, condition labels and derived predictions. The PONAR raw sensor archive is distributed by its authors.

## Reproduce the appendices and figures

From the repository root, run `python -B replay_appendices.py` with Python 3.10 or later. This standard-library replay recomputes quantities from the released records and checks them against independently generated summaries. It prints the ten held-condition rows, the condition-balanced grade recalls, and counts of the numerical checks. For a direct replay of the external experiment, use `python -B replay_ponar.py --archive PATH_TO_PONAR_V1_ZIP`. The external replay uses NumPy and scikit-learn; it was verified with NumPy 1.26.4 and scikit-learn 1.5.1. It verifies the source archive digest, reconstructs the disjoint minute blocks and dates, refits four forests, and checks all 9,620 predictions and robustness decisions across the primary and auxiliary analyses.

The four figures can be rebuilt with the following commands from the repository root (tested with NumPy 1.26.4 and Matplotlib 3.9.2).

```text
python manuscript/ieee-access/figures/make_figures.py --out regenerated
python manuscript/ieee-access/figures/make_phase_evidence.py --runs runs --out regenerated/phase-evidence.pdf
```

The first script creates `offset-bound.pdf`, `held-conditions.pdf` and `acquisition-limits.pdf`. The second draws phase-specific pilot percentiles and changes after a 0.1-s delay using `runs/u-paper-applicability/result.json` and `runs/u-paper-window-registration-diagnostic/results.json`. Its 18 phase-level descriptive statistics and all 300 differential shifts are independently reconstructed from the archived pilot panel and edge samples by `replay_appendices.py`. The markers and whiskers in the phase plot show measured medians and empirical percentiles. They do not estimate the meter's calibration uncertainty.

## Numerical records

- `runs/u-confirm-01` and `runs/u-pilot-02` contain the finite-panel primary scores and exact offset crossings. The original JSON output is preserved. Its historical secondary fields `extra_gain_and_noise_lower` and `extra_gain_and_noise_lower_by_group` contained integer overflow. Use `runs/u-followup-verification/frozen-secondary-correction.json` for that compound-error endpoint (2/300 for each panel). The primary scores and crossings were reproduced from the archived raw decimal measurements.
- `runs/u-paper-groups-01` contains the nested and archive-order folds, paired condition-level scores and independent checks. `runs/u-paper-record-order` contains the archive-index negative control and two cooler-block forward evaluations.
- `runs/u-paper-window`, `runs/u-paper-window-registration-diagnostic`, and `runs/u-paper-applicability` record the rolling-window ceilings, phase shifts, stable/flagged results, pressure-phase summaries, and simulated clipping.
- `runs/u-followup-b`, `runs/u-followup-c`, `runs/u-followup-wave-continuous`, `runs/u-wave-gain-region`, `runs/u-wave-gain-axis-audit`, and `runs/u-paper-wave-acquisition` cover mean/waveform controls and continuous measurement-error tests.
- `runs/u-ponar-public-transfer` contains primary and auxiliary second-rig scores, timestamped predictions and the independent audit.
- `runs/u-paper-window-registration-diagnostic/edge_and_phase_predictions.csv` contains the four endpoint readings needed to reconstruct the delayed two-window contrast, together with the predictions at both phases. `runs/u-paper-wave-acquisition/cycles.csv` and the JSON audit in `review/` document the separate centered-waveform timing and lower-output-limit tests.
- The primary split and the hydraulic portion of the confirmation lock are included to document exposure and the original analysis constants. The public lock omits the internal list of unrelated candidate studies. Historical input hashes identify the files used in the original analysis. The public repository includes the published endpoints, replay inputs and specified analysis programs. The standalone replay above does not depend on those paths.

The supplied records are computational results on public data. The calibration and transfer data needed for field validation are unavailable. The repository contains no metrological certificate for FS1 and no direct transfer test of the frozen three-grade rule on another physical pump.

The seven tables originally stored as compressed CSV are distributed here as plain CSV with identical decompressed bytes. Historical JSON manifests retain their original filenames and hashes. Use the repository checksum manifest for this release.
